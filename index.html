mylovely-mice

Two mice in an open field — a male and a female — each wired to a working model of its own brain. Drive one around and the panels on the right show what its neurons are doing, moment by moment.

1 · move

Click anywhere on the floor to send the mouse there, or drive it with WASD.

2 · find cheese

Watch dopamine spike the first time it finds some — then fade as the mouse learns where to look.

3 · stand still

Stop moving and the hippocampus replays the path you just took, backwards, in a fraction of a second.

The brain you rotate is real mouse anatomy from the Allen Institute atlas, and each population reproduces a published experimental result. Nothing is pre-recorded.
